Polymarket's help centre lists 39 countries where the platform is completely restricted. The United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Belgium, Malta, Australia, New Zealand, Japan and Singapore are all on it. So is the United States, on the international platform.
